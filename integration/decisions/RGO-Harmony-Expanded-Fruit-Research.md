# RGO Harmony Expanded ? Fruit Research Worksheet

Snapshot: 2026-11-09. This compact worksheet contains the 420 locations currently mapped to generic `fruit` in RGO Harmony Expanded and one dictionary of 91 distinct raw-material goods across vanilla, Just Goods 1.1, and Fruits Expanded. It includes all 53 vanilla-map RGO values, 20 Just Goods additions, and 18 FE fruit additions. Location and province IDs are retained for exact matching. Notes and suggested RGO fields are blank for research; no new assignments are made here.

World labels use vanilla origin fields/custom tags and Just Goods world tags where available. FE fruit additions without those tags use their documented 1337 crop-world classification; generic FE `fruit` is mixed. Goods with no world tag are marked `Not flagged`. `Produces food` is `Y` when the active good definition's `food` value is greater than zero, otherwise `N` (the vanilla default is zero). This follows game data rather than ordinary dietary meaning, so some non-food goods can have `Y`.

Vanilla definitions and names come from the installed EU5 1.4.5 `in_game/common/goods` files and `main_menu/localization/english/goods_l_english.yml`. Added goods come from the pinned [Just Goods 1.1 definitions and localization](../../sources/steam-workshop/3785316314-1.1/in_game/common/goods) and [Fruits Expanded goods](../../sources/steam-workshop/3821883489/in_game/common/goods/fruits_expanded_goods.txt) with its [fruit](../../sources/steam-workshop/3821883489/main_menu/localization/english/fruits_expanded_l_english.yml), [pear/citrus](../../sources/steam-workshop/3821883489/main_menu/localization/english/pear_citrus_rework_l_english.yml), and [persimmon/lychee](../../sources/steam-workshop/3821883489/main_menu/localization/english/persimmon_lychee_l_english.yml) descriptions. Location/province data uses [More Locations map definitions](../../sources/steam-workshop/3809335454/in_game/map_data/definitions.txt) and installed English localization.
